Accessible Images, SVGs, and Decorative Icons

Informative graphics require meaningful alternative text; decorative icons require empty alt="" and aria-hidden="true".

Image Rules

  • Do not include "image of" or "photo of" in alt text.
  • Every <img> must have an alt attribute (empty alt="" for decorative).
